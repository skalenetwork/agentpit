# Temporary-email domains and temp-mail MX operators the community blocklist
# (the disposable-email-domains package) does not carry, kept here by hand and
# added to it. Checked 2026-10-27. When a throwaway address slips through, add
# its domain, or the mail server its domain points to, on a line of its own.
# One domain per line; a name also covers every subdomain of it.
#
# Never add a shared mail provider (Cloudflare, Yandex, Google, Proton, Mailgun,
# Hostinger, Purelymail) or an alias/forwarding service: real people sit behind
# those, and an MX entry here marks every domain that points to it.
# disposable_allowlist.txt wins over this file, as it does over the package.
